## 🎯 Key Metrics Explained

### 1. Total Requests
- **All service requests** received by your shop
- Includes: pending, ongoing, completed
- Period-based: Today/Week/Month

### 2. Completed Jobs
- Successfully **finished** service requests
- Shows completion rate percentage
- Basis for earnings calculations

### 3. Pending & Ongoing
- **Active** - Mechanics working on them
- **Pending** - Waiting for assignment
- Real-time count

### 4. Gross Income
- **Total revenue** from all completed jobs
- Before any fee deductions
- Full amount customers paid

### 5. Net Income
- Revenue **after platform fee** (5%)
- What actually goes to shop + mechanics
- = Gross - Platform Fee

### 6. Active Mechanics
- Mechanics **available** for new jobs
- Shows available/total count
- Real-time status updates

## 🎮 How to Use

## ⚠️ Important Notes

### Earnings Formula:
```
Customer Payment = ₱X
Platform Fee (5%) = ₱X × 0.05
Net Income = ₱X - Platform Fee

From Net Income:
├── Shop (20%) = Net × 0.20
└── Mechanic (75%) = Net × 0.75

Total = Platform (5%) + Shop (20%) + Mechanic (75%) = 100%
```

### Real-time Updates:
- Dashboard refreshes automatically
- New jobs appear instantly
- Completion triggers updates
- Status changes reflect immediately
